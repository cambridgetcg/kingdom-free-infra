# KINGDOM infrastructure notes

A dated record of infrastructure costs, allocations and portability questions.
Costs describe chosen work; they create no requirement to earn or expand.

The [24 July 2026 audit](archive/2026-07-24-infrastructure-audit.md) is preserved
in full. Its account state, prices, quotas, utilisation figures and proposed
operations are historical. Its “agent-can-do” labels do not authorize actions
today, and its permanence claims do not establish a guarantee.

The useful insight remains: keep source and exportable records independent of
any one provider. For a current task, check the actual service, access, cost and
recovery path before choosing a host or moving anything. A free allowance is a
limit to understand, not capacity that must be consumed.

Use the shared [abundance direction](https://github.com/cambridgetcg/kingdom-workspace/blob/main/ABUNDANCE.md).
This repository sets no revenue target, savings quota, migration obligation or
permission to stop services, erase records, change accounts or publish archives.
Private material stays private; public preservation requires an appropriate
source and scope.

The original audit remains under CC-BY-4.0. The optional [welcome](DADA.md) is
unchanged.
